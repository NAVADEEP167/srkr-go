SRKR GO • QUICK GUIDE

User Manual

A simple guide to finding places and exploring SRKR campus.

Navigation Features Location tips
🧭

1. Find a destination

Use the main navigation card.

  1. Search for a place. Type a building, classroom, facility or campus location in the search box. You can also choose a destination from the category list below it.
  2. Choose your destination. Select the matching suggestion or dropdown option.
  3. Get your location. Tap Use My Location. If your browser asks for location permission, allow it so SRKR Go can use your current position.
  4. Start the route. Tap Find My Route to request directions to the selected destination.
  5. Follow the map. Use the displayed route and navigation guidance to move towards your destination.
Tip: Select a destination before requesting a route. Location access is needed for features that use your current position.
✨

2. Explore SRKR Go features

Open a feature from the feature launcher below the map.

📍

What's Near Me

⭐

Keep frequently used places handy.

🕘

🚧

📸

Share your SRKR campus moments.

📡

3. Location and access tips

For location-based features to work smoothly.

  • Allow location access when your browser asks for it.
  • Keep your device location service enabled while using current-location features.
  • If your position is not detected correctly, move to a place with a clearer GPS signal and try again.
  • What's Near Me checks that you are inside SRKR campus before displaying nearby places.
